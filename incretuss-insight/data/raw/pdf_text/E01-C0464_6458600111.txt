ABOUT KONZER
Weifang Konzer Safety Protective Equipment Co., Ltd., since its establishment in 2019,has
been deeply involved in the professional protective clothing field, with a product portfolio
covering cleanroom,medical, industrial, and high-level chemical protection.
Corporate Showcase
In the high-standard pharmaceutical industry,the essence of protection is building reliable
and verifiable control barriers. Therefore, we focus our core capabilities on the research
and development and manufacturing of disposable sterile cleanroom clothing.The entire
production processis completed in ISO5-7 cleanroom environments and strictly adheres to
GMP and ISO 14644 standards. Our products have comprehensive quality documentation
and validation systems. From quality stability to compliance, we consistently adhere to
stringent process requirements and quality systems, helping customers minimize the
potential risk of personnel contamination and empowering pharmaceutical aseptic
production contamination control strategies.

process from raw materials to production, environment,and
finished products has been established to ensure that every

Sterile Donning Solution
Donning Indicator Line
To ensure sterile donning for personnel,meticulous consideration was
given to the product design and packaging. Every detail is designed to
reduce the risk of human danger and ensure the efficiency and reliability
of the donning process.
Clear blue indicator line are
designed inside the waist of the
coverall to effectively prevent the
risk of contamination due to
incorrect wearing direction.
Sterile Folding
The sterile folding process makes the user only
come into contact with the inside of the cleanroom
garment after opening the packaging,minimizes
shaking and unnecessary contact, guarantees the
initial sterility of the cleanroom coverall.
Packaging Method
DISpaSABLE cOVERAL
The product is packed in double-layer
vacuum bags. The outer bag can be
removed before entering the clean area,
and the inner sterile packaging can be
opened in the donning area to effectively
isolate external contamination.
