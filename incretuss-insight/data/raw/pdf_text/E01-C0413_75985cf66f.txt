AE Reactors:
AE Reactors, also known as Flange-Type Glass-
Lined Reactors, are specially designed vessels 
widely used across the chemical, pharmaceutical, 
and allied process industries. The defining feature 
of AE Reactors is the flanged top design, which 
enhances both functionality and serviceability.
Key Features and Advantages
1.Ease of Maintenance: The flanged top 
construction provides easy access to the internal 
surface of the vessel, making maintenance 
operations quick and efficient. This design allows 
for convenient cleaning, inspection, and repair 
work without compromising safety or glass 
integrity. 
2.Exceptional Versatility: 
AE 
Reactors 
are 
designed to accommodate all types of agitators, 
enabling a wide range of process operations 
including 
mixing, 
reaction, 
distillation, 
crystallization, and more. The top flanges allow 
effortless integration of accessories, instruments, 
and process modifications to meet specific 
production requirements.

SMART SEAL©
The Protective bush is designed to fit snugly 
around the manhole cover of a glass-lined reactor, 
providing a barrier to prevent the maintenance 
hole cover from hitting the vessel.
The protective bush is made of a durable, non-
contaminating material and is designed to be

Extra Accessories 
AGITATING NOZZLE
Agitating nozzles are designed to improve the 
flow dynamics within jacketed reactors. They 
achieve this by generating turbulent flow, which 
is essential for effective heating or cooling of 
the reactor contents. The nozzles project into 
the jacket space and create a hydraulic jet that 
discharges at a right angle, promoting turbulence 
throughout the jacket. This turbulence is crucial 
for maintaining uniform temperature distribution 
and optimizing heat transfer efficiency.
