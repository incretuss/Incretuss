Vita-Q® Fat-Soluble
Vitamin Beadlets
Encapsulated fat-soluble vitamins and actives — free-
flowing beadlets and oils for nutraceutical formulations.
A water-soluble polysaccharide matrix carries the fat-
soluble active, protecting it from moisture, acidic pH,
heat, oxygen and ingredient interactions — and releasing
it where and when the formulation needs it.
The Vita-Q® range covers vitamin K2 (natural and
synthetic MK-7), D3, D2, A, DHA, CoQ10, lutein,
astaxanthin, MCT oil, biotin and resveratrol — as beadlets
and oils, at standard or customised concentrations.

STEP 1
Carry
Fat-soluble droplets are dispersed
inside a water-soluble
polysaccharide matrix and dried
into a free-flowing beadlet.
›
STEP 2
Shield
The matrix keeps out moisture,
oxygen and acidic pH, and separates
the active from reactive neighbours
in the blend.
›
STEP 3
Release
On dissolution the matrix opens
and the active is delivered where
the formulation needs it.
