MACHINERY  /  Welcome to ACG machinery

What makes ACG’s 
machinery better?
1
Global design, local support
Our multinational team of 
engineers, designers and developers 
ensures our machines are always 
at the forefront of technology. 
ACG acquired its own German 
development team in 2016, and 
also regularly collaborates with 
equipment designers in France, 
Italy, the USA, India, and Croatia.
Support teams are more local, 
however, and are always on hand 
to assist with technical, regulatory 
and any other kind of assistance 
you may require.
2
We see the bigger picture
You already know that working 
with machines involves far more 
than just the machines themselves. 
For example, it’s also about how your 
machines interact with the capsules 
or packaging materials running 
on them.
As the world’s only integrated 
solid dosage solution provider 
(having ‘Capsules’, ‘Engineering’, 
‘Packaging Materials’ and ‘Inspection’ 
divisions), only ACG can offer holistic 
and integrated advice on your 
manufacturing processes, and 
provide everything you need 
from under one roof.
3
Our parts are better
All our format and change parts 
are manufactured with the highest 
spec’d materials, in world-class 
facilities, and using the best CNC 
machines on Earth. Every part is also 
tested under live conditions so you 
can use them straight from the box.
All of the quality that goes into our 
parts is reflected in how reliably 
they perform – saving you time 
and money in the long term.

GTC III
OEB 5-compliant granulation line
The GTC III ensures maximum 
operator safety and environmental 
protection, making it ideal for 
granulating oncological, hormonal, 
biopharmaceutical, antiviral, and 
other HPAPIs.
The system integrates an HSM with 
a wet mill, fluid bed, dry mill, and 
blender for contained granulation, 
particle sizing, and final blending. 
GTC III also features multiple glove 
ports for 100% accessibility, an 
integrated weighing scale for real-
time monitoring of binder solution 
spray rates, and rapid active 
transfer ports to facilitate material 
charging in isolators without 
compromising integrity.
Strategically placed fixed and 
handheld washing nozzles ensure 
thorough and efficient cleaning, 
saving both time and effort.
The IPC-based control system 
complies with 21 CFR Part 11 and 
includes features such as audit 
trails, user management, recipe 
management, and batch reporting.
GTC III supports flexible working 
volumes, ranging from 0.6 to 6 litres.

is specially designed for use in

process results.
GPCG 1.1 
A lab and pilot-scale 
granulation system
GPCG 1.1 is one of the most trusted 
models in its performance class 
for feasibility and scale-up studies. 
Packed with all the features of a 
production-scale machine, GPCG 1.1 
is ideal for handling a wide variety 
of products.
It covers the entire process of drying, 
granulation, and Wurster pellet 
coating in a single container.
QUEST™ FB I
Facilitates pharma innovation 
while enabling seamless scale-up
This lab-scale fluid-bed system 
champions safety and precision and 
is ideal for pharmaceutical feasibility 
studies. It features a certified 12-bar 
pressure shock-resistant design,

platform, this unit maximises space 
while providing high performance 
right where you need it.
QUEST™ FB II
A versatile R&D scale granulation 
system with 12-bar pressure 
shock resistance
The ultimate lab system, performing 
drying, granulation and Wurster 
pellet coating in a single container.
The granulation system is 
accompanied by four process inserts 
to accommodate varied batch sizes 
and products. Certified 12-bar 
explosion resistance means the 
machine sets new safety standards. 
Other features include dust-free filter 
change, slide-in / slide-out process 
inserts, and plug-and-play design.

ZRO series
High yield capsule-filling machines
This series of high speed, fully 
automatic capsule-filling machines 
is available in capacities of up to

MACHINERY  /  Encapsulation

Our products
Encapsulation
FLUIDOCAP series
Automatic liquid filling in 
hard shell capsules
Easy-to-operate automatic machines 
for liquid filling. Outputs range from 
1,000 to 70,000 capsules per hour, 
and can fill with various liquids, 
pastes, suspensions, thixotropics 
and hot melts. 
FLUIDOCAP machines can handle 
formulations with viscosities of 
40 to 40,000 cP, and include ‘no-
capsule-no-fill sensors’ that reduce 
waste, plus nitrogen or inert gas 
purging for encapsulating oxygen-
sensitive formulations. They also 
fill pellets, tablets, and capsule-
in-capsule variants, and have 
technology for sealing capsules filled 
with liquids, semi-solids and solids.
AF series
Automatic capsule-filling 
technologies
This series of fully automatic 
capsule-filling machines has outputs 
ranging from 6,000 to 200,000 
capsules per hour. They’re suitable 
for capsule sizes #000 to 5, using 
tamping technology. Plus, the precise 
slug-dosing principle ensures high 
filling accuracy. 
These versatile machines allow 
multiple dosage combinations, 
such as powders, pellets, tablets, 
micro-tablets, softgel, and capsule-
in-capsule filling. They can also 
integrate seamlessly with ACCURA, 
ACG’s 100% capsule checkweigher.
All models within the series feature 
ACG Smart Connected technology.
PROSEAL
A technology for sealing hard 
shell capsules filled with liquids, 
semi-solids and solids
This band-sealing machine is ideal 
for sealing capsules filled with 
formulations comprising liquids, 
semi-solids, pastes, thixotropics, 
powders, pellets, tablets, micro-
tablets and capsules. With a 
changeover time of 260 minutes, 
PROSEAL has a highly efficient 
three-drum capsule loading and 
orientation system.
Better airflow management through 
separate blowers for suction and 
discharge ensures fast and effective 
drying of capsules, so PROSEAL has 
an output capacity of up to 70,000 
capsules per hour.
SECUREFILL series
High-level containment 
capsule-filling lines
This containment series is suitable 
for highly potent and toxic 
formulations with controlled OEL 
(occupational exposure limits), such 
as oncology, biopharmaceuticals, 
antivirals, and other HPAPIs. The 
machines are equipped with a 
negative pressure structure that 
includes RTP port, HEPA filters, 
glove ports, and accurate dosing 
mechanisms.
Meanwhile, the WIP (wet-in-place) 
system ensures wetting of all air-
borne particles in the pharma zone, 
thereby safeguarding the operator 
and environment. SECUREFILL’s 
output range is 6,000 to 40,000 
capsules per hour.
ACCURA series
100% capsule check-weighing 
systems
ACG’s capsule-checkweighers 
guarantee capsule weight accuracy, 
whether they’re empty, filled, or 
partially filled, and with anything 
from powders to pellets. Maintaining 
precise dosing safeguards the 
integrity of pharmaceutical 
production, and ensures enhanced 
product quality and compliance.
The ACCURA series of 100% 
capsule checkweighers is designed 
specifically for precise automatic 
and continuous weighing, and uses 
the latest digital technology. These 
machines are also best suited for 
medium to large production batches 
with capacities exceeding 200,000 
capsules per hour.
