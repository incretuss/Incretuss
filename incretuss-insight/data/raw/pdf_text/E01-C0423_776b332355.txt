glass packaging for pharmaceutical industry
TRADE COMPANY

SVET, JSC is not only one of the oldest factories in Russia but also 
one of the most high-tech in the country. The plant provides a full 
technological cycle of glass packaging production from the 
graphic realization of the idea to the delivery of ﬁnished products 
to the consumer.
The company's production facilities allow it to produce more than 
750 million units of glass products per year. SVET plant is the only 
enterprise in the Russian Federation that at the same time 
produces products for the needs of the alcoholic, food, chemical, 
perfume and pharmaceutical industries from colorless, brown, 
super flint (ultra flint) and colored glass. Colored glass is 
manufactured at the factory using the technology of staining glass 
in bulk at the outflow of the furnace in the feeder channel. This 
technology makes it possible to obtain almost any color of glass.
At the end of 2020, the SVET plant launched production
of products for the pharmaceutical industry. Glass containers
are manufactured using BB and NNPB technology from colorless 
and brown glass and correspond to type lll glass. The quality
of all products meets European standards.
Long-term and mutually beneﬁcial partnerships with the largest 
consumers in Russia and other countries, a wide range of 
high-quality products, a stable ﬁnancial condition is the key
to the further development of the plant. 
As the saying goes, ‘Actions speak louder than words’. This proverb perfectly suits
to the products that JSC SVET has been producing since 1835. Our company was 
founded on the basis of the Syuginsky glass factory and in 1922 this glasshouse
received its new name – SVET.
We will be glad to see you among our partners!
