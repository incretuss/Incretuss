About Us
Sabiha Research & Development Ltd. is a multidisciplinary analytical and research facility
based in Punjab, India, established in 2020. What began as an analytical testing laboratory
has grown into a nationally recognised producer of Certified Reference Materials (CRMs)
and NIST Traceable Secondary Reference Standards.
Through our NABL-accredited RMP division, we operate under ISO 17034:2016 - the
international standard for reference material producers - and supply high-purity CRMs
designed for accurate analytical work in pharmaceutical, industrial, research and testing
laboratories. 
We are ISO 17034:2016 certified for 56 Primary Reference Standards. In addition, we
supply a broad range of NIST Traceable Certified Secondary Reference Standards,
including metal standard solutions, ion chromatograph standards, and buffer and
conductivity standards.
