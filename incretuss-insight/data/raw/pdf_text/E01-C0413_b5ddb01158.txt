Agitated Nuthce Filter Dryer (ANFD) is a 
multifunctional system used for solid liquid 
separation, filtration, washing and drying of 
various materials in pharma, chemical and food 
industries. Glass lined ANFD gives high corrosion 
resistance.
Vessel: Glass lined Upper part having cylindrical 
shell, dished end on top, flange joint on bottom 
and Jacket, which gives better heating and 
Vacuum. Lower part having flat bed support for 
PTFE filter media, cloth and jacket gives better 
heating and Vacuum.
Agitator: Agitator with bellow seal allows better 
sealing, heating and mixing in centre of the 
Vessel.
Filter: ANFD filter system that separates solids 
from liquids.
Drying: Upper part and lower part provided with 
jacket and connected to external heating source 
which dries the filtered cake.
Discharge: Discharge valve provided to discharge 
cake from Vessel.
Vacuum: ANFD operates under Vacuum which 
gives faster drying of materials.
Working principle of ANFD
• Loading: Slurry or wet material loaded into 
the ANFD.
• Filtration: Agitating and heating to be done 
under vacuum. Filtration shall be carried out 
and filtered media collected. 
• Washing: After filtration the solid cake is 
washed with suitable solution to remove 
impurities and residues.
• Drying: After washing agitator is used by 
rotating to dry solid cake by heating and 
vacuum.
• Discharge: After Drying cake is discharged 
from vessel through discharge valve.
Design Conditions:
• Design Pressure: FV/4.5Kg/Cm2 (g)
• Design Temperature: 200 °C
