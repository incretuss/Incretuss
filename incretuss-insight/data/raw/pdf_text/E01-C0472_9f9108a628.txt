secreted by stimulation. They regulate cell growth, differentiation and immune response to participate in cell activity 
via binding with relevant receptors. Cytokines contain interleukins, inflammatory factors, matrix metalloproteinases, 
growth factors, chemokines, colony-stimulating factors, interferons, tumor necrosis factors etc.
