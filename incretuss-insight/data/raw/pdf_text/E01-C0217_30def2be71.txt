OUR SOLUTION

For high-containment production of highly toxic, highly active, and highly allergenic drugs, OEB4/5 protection levels are 
usually designed based on pharmaceutical process risk assessment. 
In order to meet market demand, utilizing Miran's more than ten years of accumulated high-containment isolator technol-
ogy in the pharmaceutical ﬁeld, Miran has partnered with mature OSD equipment manufacturer to jointly develop high-con-
tainment OSD line, granulator, ﬂuid bed dryer, tablet pressing machine, coating machine, etc., to meet the protection level 
requirements in drug manufacturing processes and provide users with complete high-containment OSD production solutions.
对于高毒性、高活性和高致敏性药物的密闭生产，根据制药工艺风险评估，通常设计OEB4/5防护等级。
为了满足市场需求，密朗利用在制药领域积累十余年的密闭隔离技术，与成熟的固体制剂装备企业携手，联合开发高密闭固
体制剂生产线，其中包括湿法制粒、流化床、压片机、包衣机等，以满足药品制造过程中的防护等级要求，为用户提供完整的高
密闭固体制剂生产解决方案。
