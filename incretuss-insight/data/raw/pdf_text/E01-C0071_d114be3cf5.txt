ABOUT US
Changzhou Huajian Pharm Pack Material Co., Ltd. is a high-tech enterprise 
specializing in the R&D, manufacturing, and sales of direct-contact pharma-
ceutical packaging materials. We are committed to providing safe, reliable, 
and internationally compliant packaging solutions for the global pharma-
ceutical industr
The company operates a 16,000-square-meter high-barrier intelligent clean 
production workshop (Grade C/D environments) and holds 13 Drug Packag-
ing Material Registration Numbers approved by China's Center for Drug 
Evaluation (CDE). Our product range includes pharmaceutical aluminum foil, 
composite films, punch-forming materials, and other types of pharmaceuti-
cal flexible packaging, with an annual production capacity of 32,700 tons.

In September 2020, we established a strategic partnership with Guangdong 
Dongfeng New Materials Group Co., Ltd. (SSE Main Board, Stock Code: 
601515), a listed company on the Shanghai Stock Exchange. As a comprehen-
sive new materials industry group with extensive resources in new energy, 
pharmaceutical packaging, and printing & packaging sectors, Dongfeng 
Group further enhances our capabilities in innovation, quality, and global 
supply chain management.
Huajian is recognized as a National High-Tech Enterprise, a Jiangsu Province 
“Specialized, Refined, Unique, and Innovative” SME, and a Changzhou 
Municipal Green Factory. We have established provincial-level Engineering 
Technology Research Centers and Enterprise Technology Centers. Equipped 
with first-class testing instruments such as GC-MS, Headspace Gas Chroma-
tography, FTIR, and Oxygen & Water Vapor Transmission Rate Test Systems, 
we strictly implement full-process quality management to ensure stable 
product performance, safety, and compliance.
Currently, we provide packaging support for over 300 renowned pharmaceu-
tical companies,including China Resources Sanjiu Medical & Pharmaceutical 
Co., Ltd., Dongyangguang Pharmaceutical, Jiangzhong Pharmaceutical, 
Fosun Pharma, and Beijing Tong Ren Tang. Our products are widely used in 
various solid preparations, health products, and medical device fields.
In response to the increasingly stringent regulations and quality require-
ments of the global pharmaceutical packaging industry, Huajian, guided by 
its mission of “Safeguarding Quality Medicine for Health,” adheres to the 
values of “Dedication, Effort, Co-creation, and Win-win.” Through continu-
ous technological innovation and industry-academia-research collaboration, 
we consistently enhance the protective properties and application experi-
ence of our products for pharmaceuticals.
We look forward to establishing partnerships with more domestic and 
international pharmaceutical companies to jointly promote the develop-
ment of pharmaceutical packaging toward low-carbon, environmentally 
friendly, and innovative directions.
