ELISA Kits for therapeutic mAbs and ADAs
FineTest® ELISA kits are used for the detection and quantification of therapeutic monoclonal antibodies 
such as Bevacizumab® and biosimilars including rituximab® and adalimumab®, as well as the immu-
nogenicity detection of Anti-Drug Antibodies (ADAs).
Monoclonal antibody drugs are exogenous proteins. Most of them have been modified via humanized 
heavychain, because Fab region is still immunogenic. Allergic and immune reactions often occur in 
large quantities or long-term injections. FineTest® has developed some ELISA Kits to help scientific 
researchers and medical workers to determine the metabolism of drugs during drug treatment.
