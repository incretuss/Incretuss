tions outlined in the Chinese Pharmacopoeia, the United States Pharmacopeia, the European Pharmacopoeia, and the 
Japanese Pharmacopoeia, thereby upholding the utmost in product integrity and safety.

LMH Cell Culture Media 
The LMH cell culture medium is highly effective in supporting the growth and proliferation of LMH adherent cells, facilitat-
ing efficient intracellular expression of avian adenovirus. This serum-free, protein-free and animal component-free 
medium, significantly reduces downstream purification costs. It has been widely utilized in various avian adenovirus 
vaccine production projects.
Adaptation of Adherent Cells
LMH adherent cells can be successfully adapted to the LMH SFM 777 medium, which is serum-free. These cells 
exhibit effective proliferation in the first generation and quickly adapt to the suspension culture system.
Growth of LMH Suspension Cells
Suspension adaptation of adherent LMH cells using LMH SFM 777 medium, with daily monitoring of viable cell density 
and viability.
Stable passaging of LMH suspension cells is achievable in LMH SFM 1516 medium, wherein cell densities reach 
6.0-7.0 × 106 cells/mL within 72 hours, demonstrating a notable advantage in both density and viability over compet-
ing media formulations. LMH suspension cells were seeded at a density of 1.0 × 106 cells/mL in the LMH series 
medium. The peak viable cell density in the LMH SFM 1516 medium reached 12.0 × 106 cells/mL, with a consistent-
ly maintained cell viability rate of over 95%. This favorable condition is conducive to subsequent virus infection and 
expression processes.

MDCK Cell Culture
Media 
The MDCK SFM 1415 medium facilitates rapid adaptation of adherent MDCK cells to suspension culture, while 
supporting serum-free, high-density growth of MDCK suspension cells, and enhancing the efficient expression of avian 
influenza viruses. This serum-free and animal -origin component free medium, significantly reduces costs of down-
stream purification processes.
The MDCK SFM 1607 medium enables the stable passaging of MDCK cells, with a doubling time of approximately 16 
± 1 hours. In batch culture, the MDCK SFM 1607 medium promotes high-density growth of MDCK cells, achieving a 
maximum viable cell density of 22.0 × 106 cells/mL.
Animal component-free, serum-free

F81 Cell Culture Media

reduction in serum usage to 3.0% during the cell passage and growth phase. This not only facilitates process scaling 
but also shortens cell doubling time, thereby reducing production and purification costs. Applied to F81 cell adaptation, 
passaging, expansion, scale-up, and virus production.
(a) Serial subculture of adherent F81 cells in MEM medium containing 5% NBCS, with a split ratio of 1:8 to 1:6, 
performed every three days. (b) Adaptation of adherent F81 cells to suspension culture in F81 LSM 801 medium 
supplemented with 3% NBCS, initiated at a seeding density of 1.5 × 106 cells/mL, and passaged every three days.

Vero Cell Culture Media 
Vero cells originate from normal adult African green monkey kidney tissue. Renowned for their susceptibility to a wide 
range of viruses and amenability to adaptation on microcarriers, they are extensively employed in the production of viral 
vaccines. CD VM 01 is a serum-free medium, devoid of any animal-origin components, designed to ensure excellent 
lot-to-lot consistency. It is specifically formulated to facilitate serum-free attachment culture of Vero cells and to enhance 
the efficient production of viruses.
In comparison to the control, CD VM 01 medium promotes the growth of Vero cells to higher densities while maintaining 
increased viability.
(a) Documentation of Vero cell growth in both control and CD VM 01 media as observed under an inverted microscope. 
(b) Serial subculturing of Vero cells in both control and CD VM 01 media.
