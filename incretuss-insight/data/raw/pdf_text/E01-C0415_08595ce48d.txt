Stiriti is your unique source for hard to find natural ingredients. The company based in Hyderabad,
India, has formulation and extracts manufacturing and research facilities. We are linked to worldwide
suppliers of botanicals and its parts. Our every step is focused on making your sourcing requirements
easier. Stiriti’s value added services to its customers is the ability to move ingredients hassle free
across the seas, handle imports. Firmly believing on trust and relationships, we make sure that you
feel at home with us for your sourcing needs.
Our contacts are spread all over the world for sourcing unique herbs. We have a fully verified and reliable
supply chain from the lesser-known countries in Africa to numerable suppliers from China and India. We have
the ability to source from wild-grown, cultivated, or Certified Organic herbs and botanicals.
STIRITI has established a strong partnership with a world-class laboratory in India, dedicated to the analysis
and testing of your ingredients. We offer you a comprehensive package that includes the Standard Test
Procedures (STP) along with promptly delivered test results. We encourage you to take advantage of our in-
house testing services, which are equipped with cutting-edge technologies such as HPLC, GC(H), and AAS-GF.
STIRITI provides manufacturing and custom manufacturing services in India, ensuring complete transparency
and adherence to Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). Our manufacturing facilities in India are fully compliant
with Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) and equipped with Cutting-edge pharmaceutical-grade machinery.
These facilities are fully registered With Local government authorities and have obtained certifications from
relevant regulatory agencies. We would like to extend an invitation for you to visit our manufacturing facility at
your convenience, allowing you to personally inspect our operations and engage our team. Our facility is well-
equipped to accommodate production requirements ranging from small lots to large and even extensive batch
sizes, ensuring we meet your specific production needs effectively.
There are instances where a pre-mixed exclusive formula powder becomes essential. Why, you might wonder?
Well, it alleviates concerns about surplus materials, especially since minimum quantities may apply to each
ingredient selected for your unique formula. Our aim is to ensure that you receive your customized pre-mixed
formula tailored to the specific physical requirements of your machinery. Rest assured our expertise in
pharmaceutical equipment guarantees that your blend will meet your specified criteria, including bulk density,
uniformity, particle size, moisture levels, and other essential parameters. We’ re commited to providing you
with the necessary Quality Assurance Documents for your blended product.
