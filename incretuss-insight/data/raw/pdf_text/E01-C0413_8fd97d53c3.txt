Traditional methods for producing vegetable and 
fruit powders—such as freeze drying, tray drying, 
drum drying, and spray drying—are commonly used 
worldwide but come with notable drawbacks. These 
processes are often high-cost or limited by temperature 
constraints, which can lead to the loss of essential 
aromas, nutrients, and flavors. 
 
technology overcomes these challenges 
with an innovative, cost-effective solution that combines 
drying and milling into a single, continuous process. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Operating at a low temperature of just 60°C, 
preserves the natural integrity of 
the raw materials, ensuring maximum retention of 
nutrients and aroma. 
Extensive trials on a wide range of vegetables and 
fruits—including carrots, capsicum, okra, orange 
peels, apples, and more—have shown excellent 
results. This breakthrough technology opens up 
new opportunities for the food, pigment, and FMCG 
industries to explore more efficient, sustainable, 
and high-quality processing methods.

API & Pharmaceuticals 
In the API industry, filter-pressed powders often 
contain a Loss on Drying (LOD) upto 60%, typically 
in the form of wet cakes or lumps. The traditional 
processing method involves manually transferring 
the wet cake to drying in a VTD system for 24 to 
40 hours to reduce the LOD below 1%. Once dried, 
the cake is milled to achieve the desired particle 
size. This multi-step process is labour-intensive, 
energy-consuming, and time-consuming. 
 
 
With the Schematic 
system, 
these stages are seamlessly integrated into one 
continuous operation. The system combines 
wet milling, drying, and particle size reduction 
in a single, efficient process, offering several key 
benefits; 
Faster LOD Reduction 
Achieve the required low LOD in a 
shorter time frame. 
Fine Particle Size 
Achieve a uniform particle size as 
small as 100 µm. 
Energy Efficiency 
Operate at a low temperature of 
60°C, significantly reducing energy 
consumption while keeping the 
integrity of the product 
Reduced Labor 
Minimize manual handling and 
simplify operational processes. 
Lower Product Loss 
Enhance product yield by reducing 
waste during transitions between 
processes
