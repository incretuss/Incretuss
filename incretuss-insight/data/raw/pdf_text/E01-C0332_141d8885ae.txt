03

公司始建于1987 年，是一家通过国家食品药品监督管
理局GMP、欧洲药品质量管理局CEP 认证及日本厚生省医
药品外国制造业者认定的专业甘草产品生产企业。公司致力
于甘草有效成分的研究开发、提取分离，主营甘草食品添加剂、
甘草香精香料，甘草日化品原料等。目前在青海、乌兹别克
斯坦等地共设4 家工厂。
2014 年公司在青岛依托中科院平台成立了中国唯一的专
业甘草研究院，该院在提升公司产品工艺及质量的同时也不
断推陈出新。目前公司甘草产品种类多达40 种以上，其中已
经申报获批国家专利16 项。
2016 年在国家工信部与青海省政府的大力支持下，公司
在青海等地种植甘草达3 万多亩。此项目实施保障了公司甘
草资源的同时也做到了取之于自然，还之于自然的良好发展
势态。

04

Qinghai Blicsweet Licorice Technology Development Co., Ltd was established in 1987. 
We are a professional licorice manufacturer certified by GMP and CEP and Accreditation 
certificate of foreign durg manufacturer certified by the MHLW of Japan. We are 
committed to the research and development of licorice active ingredients, extraction and 
separation. It is mainly engaged in food additives, licorice flavors and licorice ingredients 
for cosmetics etc. Currently we have set up 4 technical licorice factories in Qinghai and 
Uzbekistan.
In 2014, we started a professional licorice R&D center in Qingdao based on the platform of 
Chinese Academy of Sciences. It focus on improving our products quality and developing 
new products. For now, we have more than 40 kinds of licorice products, and got 16 
patents.
In 2016, we got a huge support of MIIT and Qinghai Government, which help us built 
a over 20² kilometers licorice planting base. This base finds a way to use the licorice 
resources well and make the sustainable development.
