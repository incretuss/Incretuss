WHO WE ARE
We are a global turnkey engineering partner delivering concept-to- 
commissioning solutions for Pharmaceutical (API and Formulations), 
Biotechnology, Chemicals and Specialty Chemicals, Food and Beverages, 
Fragrance, Paints, Petrochemicals, and Public Sector Industries.
From the first sketch of your idea to the final qualification and handover, we 
provide fully integrated, regulation-ready, and performance-driven turnkey 
facilities built on uncompromised quality and engineering excellence.
We specialize in designing and building highly critical chemical and pharma 
process facilities, including:
Bromine production plants
Advanced flow chemistry systems (PFR & CSTR)
Solvent recovery, distillation & stripping units
API, HPAPI & formulation facilities
Biotech & fermentation upstream/downstream plants
Every solution is built to meet stringent global regulatory standards including: 
USFDA, MHRA, EU-GMP, WHO-GMP, ANVISA, Schedule-M.
Leveraging decades of combined industry expertise, we understand that 
equipment selection, process accuracy, utility planning, and regulatory 
readiness form the backbone of a successful project. Our multidisciplinary 
teams work in perfect synchronization—anticipating challenges, optimizing 
process flows, and delivering world-class, audit-ready facilities that set new 
benchmarks in safety, quality, and performance.

Comprehensive Turnkey Services
We provide a complete spectrum of specialized engineering services, each 
designed to ensure compliance, efficiency, and operational excellence across 
your entire facility.
Mechanical Engineering
Our Mechanical Engineering services cover equipment selection, system integration, 
piping design, and material handling, delivering safe, reliable, and efficient solutions 
from concept to till hand over.
Fire & Safety
Holistic fire and safety engineering solutions ensuring code-compliant hydrant, 
sprinkler, and detection systems, reinforced by strategic emergency response 
planning for maximum asset and personnel protection.
cGMP Compliance
We ensure full cGMP compliance through plant modifications and upgrades. Our 
services include complete documentation (SOPs, Site Master File), validation records, 
and adherence to USFDA, EU-GMP, WHO, and Schedule-M standards.
Plant Layout Design
We design cGMP facilities with optimized flow for personnel, materials, and utilities. 
Our scalable solutions ensure regulatory compliance, proper zoning, and future 
readiness.
Capacity Design & Machinery Selection
We perform capacity assessments, process equipment sizing, and vendor 
evaluations. Our services include utility load planning, selection of automation-ready 
cGMP equipment, and IQ/OQ support.
Instrumentation & Automation
We implement advanced PLC/SCADA/DCS systems, BMS & EMS automation, and 21 
CFR Part 11 compliant solutions. Our services include IoT-enabled monitoring and 
comprehensive qualification support.
Cleanroom Design
We specialize in modular cleanroom architecture, ISO & cGMP classified designs, and 
HEPA/ULPA filtration systems. Our solutions ensure effective pressure cascade, 
contamination control zoning, and full validation.
Environmental Engineering
We design and implement Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP), Sewage Treatment Plants 
(STP), and Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) systems. Our expertise includes water recycling, 
air pollution control, hazardous waste handling, and sustainability engineering.
