Ningbo Zeinley Pharmaceutical Packaging Co., Ltd. was founded in 1991 with the mission to contribute to the health 
of all humanity. It provides diversified pharmaceutical packaging and drug delivery system solutions for global 
pharmaceutical companies. 
 
Zeinley focuses on the research and development of pharmaceutical packaging material technologies, expanding from glass 
inner packaging to combination devices and precision drug delivery systems. The company offers high-quality products such 
as injection vials, cartridge bottles, pre-filled syringes, ready-to-use (RTU) series, and innovative products made from cyclic 
olefin copolymers (COC), widely used in vaccines, biologics, cell and gene therapies, and aesthetic injections. 
 
Zeinley complies with CHP, USP, EP, JP, YBB, and ISO standards, ensuring global pharmaceutical packaging quality. 
Products are FDA DMF-listed, CE-certified, Halal-approved, and registered in India, Russia, Indonesia, and 
South Korea, with additional certifications underway.
 
With an international research team, Zeinley ensures technological innovation, providing 
safe, high-quality, and stable products and professional services to global 
pharmaceutical companies, aspiring to be a global leader in diversified 
pharmaceutical packaging and drug delivery solutions.

Dual-Chamber Injection System (DCS) is a combination device 
with two separate chambers containing a powder or lyophilized 
drug and a diluent. The drug is stored in a freeze-dried form 
to ensure long-term stability, especially for biologics. Prior to 
injection, the drug and diluent are mixed via a bypass channel 
within the glass barrel.
