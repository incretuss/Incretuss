ThermoSpheres™
Nutrient Beadlets
Heat-stable, taste-masked nutrient beadlets made for
gummies — protect, release, perform.
ThermoSpheres™ are heat-stable, taste-masked
nutrient beadlets designed specifically for gummy
applications. Precision granulation and
microencapsulation protect sensitive nutrients through
gummy processing.
Each beadlet disperses uniformly with no sedimentation
or grittiness, shields its nutrient from moisture, heat and
ingredient interaction, and delivers excellent taste and
texture in pectin, gelatin and vegan gummies.
