About us
 
STIRITI is a trusted manufacturer and supplier of premium herbal ingredients and custom
supplement solutions. With a combined experience of over 50+ years across Natural Product
Chemistry, Finance, and Business Strategy, we specialize in Herbal Extracts, Botanical
Ingredients, and tailor-made Supplement Manufacturing, including capsules, powders, and
liquids. Our capabilities span from lab-scale development to full commercial-scale production,
supported by an export-focused global sourcing network. Our Extraction Business Unit is
dedicated to high-quality herbal extraction, blending, and standardization, delivering consistent,
potent, and customizable plant-based solutions for diverse formulations. Our operations and
manufacturing processes are closely integrated with strong R&D, advanced facilities,
horticulturists, scholars, farms, and a well-structured supply chain. With Fast Turnaround,
Assured Quality, and Flexible Partnerships, we aim to collaborate on innovative formulations
and natural health solutions.
THE STIRITI ADVANTAGES
Managed by professionals with many years of experience, Stiriti is owned by qualified experts
in the manufacture of natural and botanical extracts. We offer strong technical team support
and ensure complete transparency in sharing testing methods. With the ability to supply
ingredients in any required quantity, we are equipped to meet diverse customer needs with
reliability and consistency.

Laboratory Unit
Our laboratory team comprises PhDs, Masters, and Bachelors from various scientific
disciplines. Stiriti is equipped with advanced analytical instruments such as HPLC (with
PDA), GC (with HS), and AAS, with in-house validated methods for detecting heavy metals
at ppb levels.

metabolites. The lab is well equipped with modern labware and has access to premier
scientific libraries.

characterization, and process optimization, all under strict non-disclosure agreements. We
also provide complete documentary support, with all project rights transferred to
customers.
