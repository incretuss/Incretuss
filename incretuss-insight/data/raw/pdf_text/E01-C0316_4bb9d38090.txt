NioSphere™
Niosomal Delivery
Plant-derived niosomal delivery for vitamins, minerals and
carotenoids — water- and lipid-soluble actives in one system.
NioSphere™ is a plant-derived niosomal delivery platform
built from sucrose esters, phytosterols and gum acacia.
It enhances the stability, solubility and bioavailability of
sensitive nutrients.
Water-soluble actives such as vitamin C sit in the
aqueous core; lipid-soluble actives such as vitamin D3
and lutein sit in the bilayer — in a single clean-label,
food-grade system with no phospholipids, no
cholesterol and no organic solvents.

How the niosome works
STEP 1
Build
A non-ionic, food-grade sucrose
ester bilayer forms the vesicle;
phytosterols stabilise and rigidify
it.
STEP 2
Load
Water-soluble actives and ferric
pyrophosphate sit in the aqueous
core; lipid-soluble vitamin D3 and
lutein sit in the bilayer.
STEP 3
Shield
Gum acacia nanospheres form a
steric and electrostatic shield,
protecting the actives from heat,
light and oxidation.
Proposed structure, not to scale.
