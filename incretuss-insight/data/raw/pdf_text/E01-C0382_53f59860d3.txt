MediumBank, founded in 2019, is a integrated provider of bioprocess reagents and upstream solutions for the
global biopharmaceutical industry.
Our core offerings include catalog cell culture media (CCM), AI media optimization and development (AIMD), rapid 
reagent production (RRP), Efficient Hydration Solution (EHS), and spent metabolic analysis (SMA). These solutions 
support diverse cell culture platforms, including CHO, HEK293, mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs), immune cells, and 
insect cells. 
We serve customers across diverse biopharmaceutical applications, including monoclonal and polyclonal antibodies, 
vaccines, cell therapy, and cultivated meat. Supported by two GMP-compliant manufacturing facilities (Shanghai & 
Nanjing) and scalable production capabilities, MediumBank has been trusted by more than 1,000 biopharmaceutical 
companies worldwide and has successfully passed nearly 100 customer GMP audits.
Driven by continuous innovation and customer-focused collaboration, MediumBank is committed to being a trusted 
partner for bioprocess development and manufacturing.
