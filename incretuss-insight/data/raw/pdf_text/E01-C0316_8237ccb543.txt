Crea-Q™
Soluble Creatine
Next-generation water-soluble Creatine Monohydrate
Complex — high solubility, acid stability and neutral taste.
Crea-Q™ is a patent-filed Creatine Monohydrate
Complex, designed for high solubility, enhanced stability
in acidic conditions and great taste across a wide range
of food, beverage and nutraceutical applications.
In acidic drinks, ordinary creatine cyclises to creatinine,
which has no ergogenic value. Crea-Q™ loses only ~10%
of its creatine over 120 days at pH 4.0 — versus ~65%
for standard creatine — and is 3.05× more soluble, for
clear drinks with no grit or sediment.

Why creatine fails in acidic drinks
STEP 1
The problem
In solution, creatine cyclises to
creatinine, which has no
ergogenic value. Acid and heat
speed it up — so clear drinks at pH
3–4 lose the most.
STEP 2
The usual workarounds
Overdosing 1.6–4.5×, formulating
above pH 4.5, or cold chain —
adding cost, taste load, microbial
risk or shorter shelf life.
STEP 3
The Crea-Q™ answer
A patent-filed Creatine
Monohydrate Complex that keeps
~90% of its creatine after 120 days
at pH 4.0 — with lower overage at
pH 3–4.
