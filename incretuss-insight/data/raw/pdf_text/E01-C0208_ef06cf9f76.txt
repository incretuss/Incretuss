Product Information
Trans CHO Kit Plus is an upgraded transient expression system. Its basal and feed media are chemically defined and free of serum, 
hydrolysates, or animal-derived components. The system delivers high transfection rates and protein yields, is compatible with 
multiple CHO cell lines, requires no incubation step, and supports automated workflows.
Key Benefits
Key Features
▪Simplified Workflow： A streamlined workflow with an optimized glucose formulation eliminates the need for additional glucose 
supplementation, reduces contamination risk and improves batch-to-batch consistency.
▪Enhanced Expression Efficiency：Protein yield improved by 30%, making the system suitable for a wide range of recombinant 
protein transient expression applications.
▪Improved Product Quality：Supports proper intracellular protein folding and reduces mispairing in complex proteins.
