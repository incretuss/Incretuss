01 / WHO WE ARE

Built for formulators,
not commodities.

Incretuss is the botanical ingredients arm of Incretus Pvt Ltd, built to supply formulators, brand owners and distributors with consistent, well-documented plant extracts across flavour & fragrance, nutraceutical and pharma applications.

Our sourcing and extraction choices are made SKU by SKU — matching the right method to the right actives, so buyers get a specification they can build a formulation around.

02 / OUR PROCESS

Two extraction routes.
One standard: precision.

⌁
SIGNATURE

CO2 Supercritical Extraction

Solvent-free, low-temperature extraction that preserves volatile actives and leaves no residual solvent — our lead differentiator for Ginger, Pepper, Turmeric, Cinnamon, Garcinia and Boswellia Serrata.

△

Solvent & H2O Extraction

Used for water-soluble and standardized actives such as Green Coffee, Green Tea, Ashwagandha, Tulsi and Bacopa, with defined purity and percentage grades to spec.
